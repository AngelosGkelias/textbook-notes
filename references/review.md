# Teaching and technical review

Compare the raw source, teaching specification, and actual chapter/figures. Record evidence in the existing section/project log. A compact findings table can hold concept/source, output anchor, failed criterion or check, local repair, and closure evidence. Keep teaching and technical outcomes distinguishable even when one review performs both.

## Source-to-output audit

Account for the complete assigned source scope, including graphical annotations and late material. Challenge the specification itself: consolidation, omitted visuals, and accepted status can conceal a missed objective. Inspect the claimed alternative destination when a relationship is marked redundant. Section reviews can collectively supply the full audit without rereading the entire book at integration.

## Test teachability

Attempt the specification's selected understanding actions using only the chapter and declared audience prerequisites. Cite the explanation, intermediate steps, worked application, or interpreted figure that enables the answer. Knowledge supplied mentally by the reviewer reveals a teaching gap.

Add a probe where the specification tests only recall or misses a prerequisite. Concentrate deeper probes on difficult, central, or uncertain concepts. Look for missing causal links, conditions, examples needed for application, and relationships shown but never explained. Report the failed action or omitted relationship rather than “needs more depth.” Preserve representative evidence when a section passes.

## Verify consequential results

Check important derivations, every worked example and technical figure, source corrections, and consequential supplemental claims. Group repeated routine identities. Identify whether a claim is exact within stated assumptions, approximate, empirical, or illustrative; show the relevant conditions where the student uses it.

- Reconstruct critical steps from governing definitions/equations, including intermediate substitutions and where approximations enter.
- Check domains, signs, units, normalization, initial/boundary conditions, and operating regions. Recompute nontrivial numerical results independently, including displayed intermediate values.
- Test relevant limits, invariants, counterexamples, and physical/logical plausibility. Substitute solutions back into governing relations where useful.
- Compare prose, equations, figures, and results for agreement. A calculation using the wrong topology or convention is still wrong.

Choose subject-specific evidence rather than an exhaustive checklist: truth tables for finite logic, node/terminal traces for circuits, interleavings for concurrency, invariants for algorithms, or normalization/conditioning checks for probability. For plots verify data/model, axes, units, parameters, and representative points; for diagrams verify connections, directions, state/event order, and labels. Graphic polish is separate from semantic correctness.

Retain nontrivial derivations, scripts, traces, or reference passages as evidence. Use symbolic tools or simulation only when they add confidence and their assumptions/model are valid. An arithmetic check of the writer's final formula does not verify its derivation. For source corrections and researched additions, follow [authoring.md](authoring.md).

## Independence and repair

When authorized and available, use a bounded independent reviewer for difficult teaching judgments, consequential corrections, complex derivations, or unresolved disagreement. Supply raw evidence and audience assumptions without a desired verdict; see [execution.md](execution.md). Otherwise conduct a separate source-first review and alternate-method check or re-derivation for hard results, disclosing self-review.

Repair the failed criterion and affected dependents; update the specification if it caused the gap. Recheck those results and rendered pages. Broaden review only when a finding exposes a systematic problem or changed dependency. Leave unsupported results explicitly unresolved rather than treating agreement or an unfinished check as a pass.
